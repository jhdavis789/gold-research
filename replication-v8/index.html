Does matching annual returns sacrifice daily fit?

Tracking R² uses the actual portfolio return, including its scale and drift. Zero means the stock’s historical mean return would track equally well; negative values mean it would track better. Each horizon compounds the same paid account.

What exposures did the model learn?

Metal delta includes the funds and modeled option legs. Treasury weight is the actual TLT holding as a fraction of pre-trade capital. Gamma describes curvature of the hypothetical portfolio at each trade, rather than proving a company’s operating structure.

Can permitted instruments explain the shared residual?

Peer PCA describes common company movement, with the target excluded from its own component. A second test fits that shared residual using past observations of permitted fund and option movements. Its signed statistical coefficients and intercept do not form a paid portfolio.

Explanatory only: contemporaneous market movements, coefficients frozen quarterly. Poor translation does not prove the residual cannot be hedged with other instruments or better data.
