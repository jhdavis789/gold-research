# Translating shared company moves into permitted instruments

This diagnostic was specified while the paid twenty-company batch was running,
before viewing its diagnostic outcomes. Reuse the preserved v7 seven-company,
leave-target-out one-PC contribution: one-PC prediction minus baseline
prediction. The target company never enters its own peer component. Coefficients
in that source were fitted with past data; contemporaneous peer returns supply
the realized explanatory response. This is not a forecast or a permitted holding.

At quarter ends, fit the previous 504 daily observations of this response onto
permitted gold, silver and Treasury fund excess-return movements, with optional
broad equities and/or the sixty modeled option-structure value increments.
Use an intercept and fixed ridge penalty 0.01 on training-RMS standardized
coordinates. No peer observations enter the new regressors. Fit all four
definitions on identical dates, freeze coefficients for the following quarter,
and evaluate tracking R² and correlation. Option increments come from the
quarterly holding-segment bank; normalize to its entry underlying price.

These signed, unconstrained statistical coordinates do not imply fundable
positions, transaction costs or wealth. They test whether permitted market
movements explain the shared residual, before attempting a paid hedge. Report
every result, including negative R², rather than converting explanatory fit to
an earned return. Retain sample dates, fit cutoffs, source hashes, coefficients,
training-nesting checks and future-response perturbation checks.
