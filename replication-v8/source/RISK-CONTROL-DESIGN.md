# Past-volatility gold comparison

Add a descriptive risk control after the first four paid-company outputs were
available. This does not select or optimize a replication model. At each monthly
or quarterly execution date, use the previous 252 common sessions, ending at the
previous close. Gold weight is the sample standard deviation of stock excess
returns divided by that of gold excess returns, both relative to BIL. Limit the
weight to zero through three. Quantities remain fixed until the next scheduled
trade. Use the same paid engine, dates, cash and borrowing terms as the other
models. No future-window volatility matching is permitted. Report realized
portfolio volatility separately: past risk targeting does not guarantee equal
future volatility. This supplies two additional controls per company, separately
from the thirteen fitted definitions.
