# Conditional exposures, option basis and multiple horizons

User direction: learn companies from price behavior without requiring a fundamental operating model. Daily observations identify exposure and convexity; monthly/quarterly trades are the implementation. Evaluate daily, medium (three-month) and annual returns. Include an explicit Treasury-duration position and exclude mining peers/baskets from portfolio instruments.

Freeze this exploratory edition before viewing outcomes. Preserve v7 and its September 30, 2026 snapshots/cutoff. Twenty-company survivor universe, matched sample across all candidates per stock; at least 1,008 prior common sessions before evaluation. GLD/SLV/BIL are funded bullion/cash proxies, TLT an explicit long-duration rates holding, SPY a separately labeled extension. No exact futures-roll claim. Primary focus WPM; report every candidate and company.

For each monthly/quarterly execution calendar build a paid long-call/long-put basis on each metal: strikes 80%,100%,120% of entry spot and tenors 3,12,24 calendar months (36 choices). European Black–Scholes illustrative daily marks use GVZ for gold and trailing 63-session annualized silver realized volatility times1.25 for silver. Before GVZ begins in2008, gold uses contemporaneously available trailing realized volatility times1.25, never a later GVZ quote; first63 sessions have fixed initialization assumptions. GVZ is a30-day gold implied-volatility index, not an observed strike/tenor surface. No fabricated execution-history claim. Roll all selected options only on scheduled dates, settle intrinsic value at expiry, hold physical fund and option quantities between rolls. Compare 5% option-mark transaction costs with10% stress; funds2bp/10bp; cash BIL; borrowing BIL+50bp; short TLT1% annual assumed fee. Dividends implicit in adjusted-price units.

Bounds: gold0–3, silver0–1.5, TLT−0.75–0.75, optional SPY0–1; long options0–2 underlying-notional each, gross underlying/fund notional≤5, premium budget≤30% of capital. Premium constraint at decision uses prior-session volatility/rates; execution quantities are reduced if actual marked premiums exceed budget. Such scaling is recorded. Gold-only remains feasible in every fit; constraints applied during optimization, not arbitrary post-fit gross scaling.

Conditional local estimation weights the last1,008 observations with252-session recency half-life, additionally by similarity to observable market state at the previous close. Fixed states:126-session gold trend,63-session gold realized volatility,21-session BIL-implied annual cash yield, target's past126-session gold beta. Standardize using training observations only. Gaussian similarity exp(−0.5 mean squared standardized distance), floor0.25. Past state for each daily training return is its segment entry's previous close. No target future return or contemporaneous peers in input. Report effective sample size.

Training uses hypothetical past holding segments matched to the execution calendar and identical option basis. Fund/option quantities are constant within each segment. Its pre-cost, cash-funded segment value is affine in positions; daily returns are ratios of successive segment values, and rolling three-month/year returns compound those daily returns. Daily-only loss normalizes squared daily error by training target variance. Balanced loss uses0.50 daily,0.25 three-month and0.25 annual normalized squared errors. It is not a profit maximization objective. Borrow/short/trading fees are included in final paid replay, not fitted pre-cost templates; disclose this difference. Reuse daily price moves within annual targets, not fictitious independent annual samples. Regularization0.01 variance-normalized squared non-gold notional (options2x) discourages redundant put/call positions.

At every fit retain the feasible gold baseline and previous fit, then bounded constrained optimization; choose lowest finite feasible training criterion. Nonconvex return-ratio objectives have no global-optimality guarantee. Keep solver statuses/fallbacks. Loading constraints and fitted weights strictly precede execution; fit cutoff=i−1. Nested feasibility does not imply better future fit. All paid models are checked for positive capital.

Ten definitions × two calendars =20 portfolio rules: metals daily; metals+rates daily; conditional metals+rates daily; metals+rates balanced; conditional metals+rates balanced; metals+rates+options daily; conditional metals+rates+options daily; metals+rates+options balanced; conditional metals+rates+options balanced; metals+rates+SPY+options conditional balanced. Optional basis-bank prices are hypothetical, not exchange-verified. Daily/monthly/quarterly/annual R², absolute return gaps, wealth/costs, effective sample size and fitted delta/gamma/vega/rates exposure identify behavior.
