# Basket and lifecycle study

Freeze before calculation: equal-dollar stock sleeves at entry, either buy and hold or monthly equal-weight rebalance; cash when no eligible sleeve. Existing twenty-company dashboard supports arbitrary selection. Membership begins at the existing model-evaluation date, not IPO; this deliberately gives stock and replica the same opportunity set. No future coverage screen. Replicas retain their original internal trade rules; a basket rebalance buys/sells units of those ongoing accounts. Additional basket turnover cost is selectable (0/2/10bp) and applied to both sides. This is an account-of-accounts simulation, not a fresh per-company fit.

Realized top/bottom decile/quartile groups are hindsight diagnostics: ceil(n*p) names, equal initial dollars; median is an interpolated P50, not a held security. Percentile thresholds use linear interpolation. Annualize only after aggregation. Rolling cohorts use the same starting constituents and preserve terminal outcomes; no future constituents.

Lifecycle sensitivity adds SAND, KL, AUY, GG, GSS and Great Basin Gold. FactSet daily total returns and prices are private licensed inputs. Public output contains aggregate cohort research results and source-linked lifecycle evidence, not downloadable individual vendor histories. Existing raw Yahoo inputs remain private as before. No public redistribution entitlement is inferred from API access.

Acquisitions: mark contractual consideration at completion-date acquirer close (cash plus ratios), liquidate share consideration at that mark into BIL; actual shareholder settlement/execution can differ. Do not append full acquirer history to predecessor. GSS gets $3.91. Great Basin: receivership June28,2013 ends new eligibility; terminal recovery is not verified. Show explicit zero-recovery and last-quoted-value cash scenarios at receivership; neither is claimed as an observed final payout. No arbitrary ANV ticker reuse; Allied Nevada omitted because correct security history/recovery warrants unresolved.

For new model research use unchanged daily recency metals, metals+market and gold-only rules, 37 monthly observations warmup; SAND price history begins at confirmed US listing Aug20,2012, KL Aug16,2017, removing predecessor/OTC backfill. Other securities from2007. Compare fixed annual entry cohorts on first common session on/after Jan1 (2011–2025), hold through Sep30,2026. Use same original20 plus additions where eligible; show counts and start dates. Quantile displays for this supplemental study are ex post. This selected 26-name sensitivity does not reconstruct the historical industry and cannot eliminate survivorship or selection bias.
