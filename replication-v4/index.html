Does the portfolio track daily moves?
The original email’s R² used monthly returns. Here, daily and monthly measurements come from the same daily-valued portfolio, with the same holdings and trading schedule. Changing frequency changes measurement, not trading. The two new daily-calibrated techniques also fit on daily returns; the original techniques retain monthly calibration.


Tracking R² by company
1 − Σ(company return − portfolio return)² / Σ(company return − mean company return)². Perfect tracking is 1; below zero means worse squared error than the company’s sample mean. This does not refit the portfolio.
Regression R² is the squared correlation from a separate in-sample line with an intercept. Slope and intercept describe that line; they are not tradable profits. Tracking error is annualized with √252 for daily and √12 for monthly. Daily data adds observations, not independent long-term evidence. Close timing, stale quotes and company-specific jumps still matter.

Research assumptions and limits
  • GLD and SLV adjusted-close exposure plus BIL cash are funded bullion proxies, not a validated rolled-futures implementation. USD listings and vendor dividend/split adjustments are used.
  • Fitted exposures use trailing 24 monthly returns (risk targeting: 36), ending a full month before entry. Gold exposure is capped at 3×. Silver and paid gold options use the prior study’s fixed constraints and penalty. No peer-company return enters the portfolio.
  • Quantities remain fixed between monthly or calendar-quarter trades. Fund trades cost 2 basis points; borrowing adds 50 basis points per year. Illustrative options pay a 5% premium spread and use Black-Scholes/GVZ marks, not observed execution prices.
  • Revised vendor histories and a selected surviving universe can bias results. The companies’ business mix changes over time. No pre-listing histories are fabricated.
  • Long-horizon returns are ratios of the ongoing portfolio’s NAV, with costs already reflected. They do not reset a fresh account at each point. Selected dates restrict observations, not training history.
  • Daily observations require aligned stock/fund quotes. Only GVZ is forward-filled. Missing-session gaps, if present, are omitted from daily statistics; monthly observations still represent full month-end changes.
