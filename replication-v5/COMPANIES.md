# Company economics behind the experiments

These filings motivate hypotheses. Their current numeric disclosures were not backfilled into historical trading decisions. Return attribution to an event is an interpretation of timing, not a causal proof or a forecast.

## Wheaton: changing metal mix and fixed stream payments

Wheaton reported 2025 revenue split of 62% gold and 36% silver. Most operating-stream revenue in Q4 came from contracts with fixed per-ounce production payments. Silver must therefore be modeled explicitly; a gold-only beta omits an important asset exposure. Fixed payments create operating leverage, but the company owns long-lived contracts and growth opportunities, not a succession of three-month at-the-money calls. This motivated the 12- and 24-month option tests. Both the tenor mapping and flat-volatility pricing remain hypotheses.

Source: [Wheaton 2025 results, March 12, 2026](https://www.wheatonpm.com/news/news-details/2026/Wheaton-Precious-Metals-Announces-Record-Annual-Revenue-Earnings-and-Cash-Flow-for-2025/).

## Newmont: operating costs, currency and execution

Newmont’s annual report describes USD metal revenue and material operating costs in local currencies. Mining costs, asset changes and financing can change the equity’s metal sensitivity. The model therefore tests recent daily exposures and a separately identified oil/currency extension; a statistical currency coefficient is not proof of a specific cash-cost hedge.

Source: [Newmont 2025 annual report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1164727/000110465926035231/nem-20251231xars.pdf).

## Franco-Nevada: royalty economics and asset concentration

Franco-Nevada has precious-metal, other-mining and energy royalty/stream interests. Its 2025 financial statements show energy as a distinct revenue source. Cobre Panamá production stopped in November 2023 and stream deliveries ceased. A low corporate operating-cost base does not remove dependence on mine operators or legal permissions. Adding an oil exposure tests one economic component, but cannot recreate an asset-specific shutdown.

Sources: [2025 financial statements](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1456346/000110465926032133/fnv-20251231xex99d3.htm), [2023 annual disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1456346/000155837024003443/fnv-20231231xex99d1.htm).

## Equinox: a changing asset and financing base

The Calibre business combination closed June 17, 2025; production guidance had already changed to reflect the combination and a slower Greenstone ramp-up. A fixed multi-year beta treats materially different asset portfolios as the same company. Shorter windows and recency weighting test adaptation without using future knowledge of the transaction. SA and NG, by contrast, remain labeled developers in the universe: their equity exposure includes project financing and development probabilities, not merely current producing-mine margins.

Sources: [combination completion](https://www.equinoxgold.com/news/equinox-gold-and-calibre-mining-complete-business-combination/), [June 11, 2025 guidance](https://www.equinoxgold.com/news/equinox-gold-provides-updated-2025-gold-production-and-cost-guidance/).

## Large residuals are retained, not edited out

The market-augmented model’s largest Wheaton residual falls on December 14, 2018, immediately after the company announced settlement of its Canadian transfer-pricing dispute. The model cannot receive the positive value of that tax settlement from gold/silver holdings. Newmont’s October 24, 2024 miss coincides with its quarterly results and earnings call. Equinox’s September 19, 2023 miss follows the convertible-note offering announcement. SSR Mining’s February 13, 2024 miss coincides with the Çöpler heap-leach incident. These examples establish economically plausible omitted company risks; they do not explain every residual or establish the fraction causally attributable to each event.

Sources: [Wheaton settlement, December 13, 2018](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1323404/000127956918002473/ex991.htm), [Newmont quarterly results](https://www.newmont.com/investors/news-release/news-details/2024/Newmont-Reports-Third-Quarter-2024-Results/default.aspx), [Equinox convertible offering](https://www.equinoxgold.com/news/equinox-gold-announces-bought-deal-offering-of-convertible-senior-notes/), [SSR incident update](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/921638/000092163824000037/ssrminingprovidesanupdateo.htm).

## Convexity interpretation

For a simplified operating asset, value resembles discounted production times (metal price minus costs), less debt, with an equity floor at zero. Near the floor, limited liability and the option to defer production can matter. Well above the floor, much of the daily exposure is locally linear. Paid options also introduce time decay and volatility exposure that the equity may not share. A convincing improvement must survive later returns and premiums; a better fitted nonlinear curve alone is insufficient. The tests here assess specific finite-tenor calls, not every geological, real-option or production-cost model.
