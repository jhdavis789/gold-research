The companies explain why a gold beta is incomplete

Wheaton: metal mix and stream economics

Gold generated 62% of 2025 revenue, silver 36%. Fixed per-ounce payments create operating leverage, but these are long-lived contracts. The test adds silver and checks 12- and 24-month calls instead of assuming short ATM options are the right match.

What the convexity tests say

Paid 3-month ATM, 12-month ATM, and 12-/24-month in-the-money gold/silver calls did not reliably improve the later fit over the simpler daily metals model. Removing the penalty on extra exposure did not rescue the result. The rolling quadratic explanatory model also failed to establish a consistent gain. Oil and currency additions were mixed.

This narrows the result to the tested contracts and pricing assumptions. It does not establish that mining equity lacks convexity. Long-lived reserves, shutdown options, financing and development milestones are different claims from these finite-tenor options. A useful next research step would connect filing-known production, cost, debt and reserve changes to exposure estimates; that historical company panel is not implemented here.

The uncertainty range resamples paired monthly blocks of daily squared errors. It describes sensitivity to historical time blocks; it does not correct for specification search, vendor revisions or survivor selection.

Audit and reuse

The accounting retains cash, funded positions, paid premiums, borrowing, short-fund fees and execution costs. Quantities stay fixed between scheduled trades. Gold/silver ETF returns remain bullion proxies, and options remain hypothetical marks. No miner index, peer stock, free quadratic payoff or fitted intercept enters a portfolio.

Checked: all 580 paths and daily-to-monthly compounding; independent cash/share replay; future-data perturbations; zero-option equivalence; bounds, trade calendars and higher-cost outcomes. One platform numerical warning was investigated; the same declared least-squares objectives were rerun with a bounded quadratic optimizer and the warning-producing outputs preserved privately.
