Accounting and data limits

All new accounts include financing and short fees charged by elapsed days. Earlier v5 results understated those fees; use the corrected overlapping controls here. Quantities are fixed adjusted total-return units, which include dividend reinvestment. They are not a verified physical-share and cash-dividend ledger.

Fund costs: 2bp per trade, stressed to 10bp on stock and replica. Debt: cash proxy plus 50bp annually. Short funds: modeled 1% annual fee. These are funded bullion and other fund proxies, with no validated rolled-futures or observed-option execution claim.

Hourly history covers about two years. Five-minute raw coverage is 37 sessions and shrinks after complete positive-volume grid checks. Long histories vary by company. Historical exploration, repeated model trials, overlapping holding periods and survivor selection limit inference.

The results include financing, trading and short fees. Historical intraday observations come from Yahoo; FactSet tick history was unavailable. Methods includes the accounting and causal checks.
