# Follow-up definitions after the first frequency tests

The first tests show that within-session exposures vary with sampling frequency and that intraday-only calibration does not consistently improve daily portfolio tracking. Freeze two corrective hourly rules before computation: one-bar lead/lag regression (sum the three coefficients for each fund to estimate its longer-run response), and a 50/50 exposure blend of this rule with the matched daily126 estimate. The lead and lag observations are entirely in the past training sample, inside each session. Constrain deployed summed exposures to the existing physical fund bounds. Two instrument sets and monthly/quarterly trades produce eight follow-up rules. Keep every result and distinguish this stage from the original sixteen hourly rules. A richer explanatory regression need not produce a better investable fit after summing coefficients.

Also freeze three permitted-asset PCA experiments. Use the seven v5 assets (GLD, SLV, SPY, TLT, USO, FXA, FXC) with 756-session/126-session-half-life estimates. Keep a free gold coordinate, and constrain the other six physical weights to the span of their leading two, four or all six prior standardized-return principal components. The free gold coordinate preserves gold-only feasibility. Apply v5 physical bounds, total absolute notional cap5, variance-scaled extra-weight penalty, quarterly trades and paid v5 short/financing costs. All six components are a matched full-factor control. Components are mapped to actual permitted fund positions, not peer-stock factors. This tests whether reducing noisy directions helps the already-tested broad factor portfolio.

These are sequential exploratory candidates following the first-stage results. No candidate is promoted as independently validated by selecting its full-history rank.
