Do high margins or multiples predict worse returns?

Annual SEC filings anchor the test. Entry follows the filing; “high” and “low” are ranked against the issuer’s prior annual observations. The outcome is the stock’s future annualized return minus volatility-targeted gold.

One observation per annual filing, not twelve monthly copies. Within-issuer ranks need three earlier observations. Shared future periods and the small issuer sample limit inference. GAAP net margin is not an AISC margin.

The supply response is gradual in the latest evidence

World Gold Council / Metals Focus estimates show first-half 2026 mine output up about 3% from a year earlier. Reported industry AISC rose 16% year over year in Q1 to $1,785 an ounce. Higher prices can fund new projects, but rising costs, grades and development times also matter. These aggregate observations do not prove that high company margins cause future underperformance.

Source: World Gold Council, Q2 2026 supply review, July 30, 2026. Limited statistics reproduced for research commentary.

What the test can establish

  • The main implementation is funded GLD/SLV exposure, a cash return proxy and borrowing. It is not a verified rolled-futures backtest.
  • Gold option prices are hypothetical Black-Scholes marks using GVZ. Silver options use lagged realized volatility with fixed premium assumptions. Execution quotes and full historical volatility surfaces are unavailable. These short-life ATM and out-of-the-money structures do not represent every possible long-dated or production-cost-linked claim.
  • Positions trade monthly or quarterly. Fund trades cost 2 basis points; borrowing adds 50 basis points annually; options pay a 5% premium spread. Costs and option pricing sensitivities are retained.
  • The universe contains surviving stocks. Vendor history can be revised. Most price tests cover 15–17 years; OR has a shorter history. Historical fundamentals cover fewer years.
  • Candidate definitions and unsuccessful results are preserved. More tools were added after the initial evaluation in response to the research scope; this is a historical simulation, not live forward evidence.
