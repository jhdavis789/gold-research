# Sequential Wheaton duration hypotheses

After the completed core and both-metal option analyses, root requested a separate search for a useful WPM duration exposure. Freeze these hypotheses before their evaluation: funded gold+silver, and funded gold+silver+TLT, monthly/quarterly rebalance, 36/60-month lookback. These eight retained families test a longer estimation span and a long Treasury duration component. TLT is a registered observed fund return, not a fitted rate credit, miner index or peer stock. Historical metal revenue mix has not been acquired and is not invented.

Keep the core self-financing simulator, target costs, lagged training, fixed quantities, financing, gold cap3, extra fund caps1 and 0.0002 squared extra-weight penalty. TLT weights are long-only to avoid unverified bond short borrow. Embed the optimized unpenalized gold+silver same-window baseline in the TLT family at zero TLT. Retain every candidate and fit; no model is selected from the held-out outcomes. Each duration comparison has the same calendar and costs as its gold+silver comparator.

Save separate outcomes, never replace the existing dashboard dataset. Report both longest feasible results and a common-start replay across all eight hypotheses so a 60-month estimate cannot gain an unfair date comparison. Rolling 1/3/6/12/36/60/120-month windows open and close fresh accounts with costs. These hypotheses are sequential research on revised vendor histories, not a pristine independent validation.

Calendar correction: both controls and TLT additions trade quarterly on March/June/September/December, with the fresh initial trade before the next scheduled quarter. Training follows the same calendar; the common-start comparison remains explicit across lookbacks.
